# Trina Solar Vertex TSM-NEG19RC.20 Datasheet Summary

## Overview
The **TSM-NEG19RC.20** is a **Bifacial Dual Glass Monocrystalline Module** designed for high efficiency, durability, and compatibility with large-scale solar projects. This document summarizes the datasheet, including all specified values and estimated technical specifications based on industry standards for similar modules.

## General Specifications
- **Product Name**: TSM-NEG19RC.20
- **Type**: Bifacial Dual Glass Monocrystalline Module
  - **Bifacial**: Generates electricity from both front and back sides, capturing reflected light (albedo).
  - **Dual Glass**: Glass on both sides enhances durability and environmental resistance.
  - **Monocrystalline**: Uses single-crystal silicon cells for higher efficiency.
- **Power Range**: 580–605 W
- **Maximum Power Output**: 605 W
- **Positive Power Tolerance**: 0 to +5 W
- **Maximum Efficiency**: 22.4%
- **Temperature Coefficient**: -0.30%/°C
- **Back-Side Power Gain**: Up to 30% (depending on albedo)
- **Mechanical Load**:
  - Positive Load: 5400 Pa (e.g., snow load)
  - Negative Load: 2400 Pa (e.g., wind suction)
